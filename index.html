Rules

Core Rules

▼
  • Check: Your King is under attack — you must escape immediately.
  • Checkmate: King in check with no escape = game over, opponent wins.
  • Stalemate: No legal moves but King is not in check = draw.
  • Capture: Move onto an opponent's square to remove their piece.
  • Promotion: A Pawn reaching the far end becomes any piece — usually a Queen.
  • Castling: King moves 2 squares toward a Rook; the Rook hops over. Only if neither has moved and the path is clear.
  • En passant: A Pawn that advances 2 squares can be captured by an adjacent enemy Pawn as if it only moved 1.
Strategy

Beginner Tips

▼
  • Control the center — pieces placed in the center control more of the board.
  • Develop early — get your Knights and Bishops out in the opening.
  • Castle quickly — tuck your King behind pawns for safety.
  • Don't repeat moves — moving the same piece twice in the opening wastes tempo.
  • Think before moving — always check if your opponent has any threats first.
Piece Values

Relative Strength

▼
  • ♙ Pawn — 1 point. Building blocks of your position.
  • ♘ Knight — 3 points. Tricky, excels in closed positions.
  • ♗ Bishop — 3 points. Long-range, excels in open positions.
  • ♖ Rook — 5 points. Powerful on open files and in the endgame.
  • ♕ Queen — 9 points. Most powerful piece — guard it well.
  • ♔ King — Infinite. The game ends if it's checkmated.
